SofronPack atmosphere module\n\nThe atmosphere renderer implementation in SofronPack was written independently for Minecraft 26.3. It does not include the source code, textures or binary files of Polytone.\n\nThe default sky and fog palette values were adapted from Kev's Earth & Sky v1.2, supplied by the project maintainer. Original preset concept and visual palette: Kev.\nhttps://modrinth.com/resourcepack/kevs-earth-sky\n\nThe Polytone mod and its extensive customization API are by the Supplementaries Team, led by MehVahdJukaar.
